## Why and on which legal basis

- **Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):** to provide the service you signed up for.
- **Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and keep logs.

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not profile you and we do not sell data.

## Cookies

We only use strictly necessary cookies, so there is no consent banner:

- the session cookie, which keeps you logged in;
- `XSRF-TOKEN`, which protects forms against cross-site request forgery;
- `remember_web_…`, only if you tick "Remember me".

There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies. The dark-mode choice on the public pages is kept in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us.
